For years, our recommendations have worked in the background, inferring what a member might enjoy from their viewing history. AI Discover moves from that silent ranking toward steerable discovery. A member picks a theme, say “Quick Watches,” and gets a row of matching titles, a short note on why they fit and a few narrower themes to try next. From there, they can keep narrowing, head somewhere new or tune what they see.
