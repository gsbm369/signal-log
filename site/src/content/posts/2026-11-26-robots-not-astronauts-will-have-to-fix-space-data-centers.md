Yesterday we explored some of the difficulties facing orbital data centers in our look at the bottleneck of launching enough solar panels and radiative cooling systems to power the AI computing boom in space. JPl physicist Dr. Slava Turyshev, who wrote the paper at the center of that article, also published a follow-up study in August, available in preprint on arXiv, about how getting the hardware off the ground is only half the battle. Once all that equipment is up there, someone, or something, is going to have to maintain it.
