Joining the FinextraTV studio, Sean Tyrer, CEO and Founder, Money Carer helped to contextualise some of the key obstacles to traditional payments and banking that vulnerable adults face. Tyrer explains how, in the race to make payments faster, vulnerable adults are often left out of the equation, emphasising the need for optionality more than just assumed speed and innovation. He further describes how technologies like biometric cards and wearables are a strong way for banks and PSPs to continue evolving and innovating their offerings, while ensuring an inclusive product base.
