Google today expanded Gemini's capabilities, allowing it to take full advantage of your Mac. Gemini for Mac can use apps, files, and websites to complete multi-step tasks in the background while you work in the foreground. According to Google, Gemini can do repetitive work, handling clicking, typing, menus, and forms. It works in an isolated window with a live preview in the Gemini chat interface so you can keep an eye on what it's doing. You can choose the apps that Gemini can use, and it will ask for approval before taking "key actions." Google suggests Gemini's new feature is useful for batch editing photos, preparing listings for resale platforms, and turning metrics and screenshots into a presentation.
