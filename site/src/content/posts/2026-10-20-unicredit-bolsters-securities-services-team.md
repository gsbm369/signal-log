UniCredit today announced the next milestone in the evolution of its securities services operating model, with the planned transfer of a dedicated business unit and the related hiring of 54 specialist colleagues from Société Générale Securities Services (SGSS) SpA in Italy to UniCredit, which is expected to become effective not earlier than January 2027, subject to completion of the applicable procedures and implementation steps.
