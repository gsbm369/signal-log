At Payments Unleashed in London, Fred Slevin, Global Head, Solution Consulting, ACI Worldwide, joined FinextraTV to discuss where banks often underestimate execution beyond strategies. Slevin explained how many banks are good at developing strategies, but do not effectively appreciate the requirements needed to turn strategies into actions. Slevin believes the future belongs not to the banks with the biggest budgets or the grandest ambitions, but to those with a clear plan and the ability to make it happen.
