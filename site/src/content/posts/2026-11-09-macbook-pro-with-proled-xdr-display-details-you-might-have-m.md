Given these models are available with the same M5 Pro and M5 Max chips that power the models with mini-LED displays, performance was expected to be virtually identical across the board, and benchmark results confirm that is the case. Average scores for multi-core CPU performance in the Geekbench 7 database : 16-inch MacBook Pro (Mini-LED, M5 Max): 34,988 16-inch MacBook Pro (PROLED XDR, M5 Max): 34,904 The chip has an 18-core CPU in both models.
