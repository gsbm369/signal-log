Hackers used some of the world's most popular websites to distribute malware designed to steal cryptocurrency and exploit vulnerabilities in iPhones and iPads this week, according to a new security investigation. Reuters reports that websites associated with 178 organizations were affected by the attack on Monday, November 16, including the likes of Zoom, BBC, CNN, HSBC, Salesforce, and IKEA. A vulnerability was reportedly exploited in Optimizely , a third-party platform that companies use to test and customize their websites. Hackers managed to insert malicious JavaScript into Optimizely's customer projects, allowing them to distribute the malware through legitimate websites without directly compromising their infrastructure.
