A cruise ship spends its days tied up in port, burning diesel just to keep the lights on. The Viking Libra is designed to keep them on for its 998 guests for about 10 hours using a fuel much more benign for the environment: hydrogen. The Libra is actually the world's first hydrogen-powered cruise ship, and Fincantieri just handed it over to Viking in Ancona, Italy.
