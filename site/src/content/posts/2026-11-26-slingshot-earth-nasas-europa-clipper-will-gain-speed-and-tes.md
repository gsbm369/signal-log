NASA's Europa Clipper launched in October 2024, on its way to the Jupiter system. But it hasn't left the inner Solar System yet. It used a gravity braking maneuver at Mars in March 2025 to direct it back toward Earth. In a few days, it will use this encounter with Earth to boost its speed toward Jupiter and its moon Europa. This upcoming flyby is also a final opportunity to calibrate the spacecraft's instruments before its long cruise phase.
