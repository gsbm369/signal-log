For years, whimsically minded space enthusiasts have been dreaming of sailing across the vastness of the void on beams of sunlight. Photons, the massless particles that make up those beams of sunlight, still carry momentum, and can therefore impart a gentle push when they bounce off a surface. This force is the concept behind “solar sailing”, which has been having a bit of a moment in the last decade as several proof-of-concept flights have shown it is indeed actually feasible. Now a new paper, available in pre-print on arXiv from researchers at the Rochester Institute of Technology (RIT), has unveiled a clever technique that will allow these giant sails to steer sideways using a technology more commonly associated with flat-screen displays.
