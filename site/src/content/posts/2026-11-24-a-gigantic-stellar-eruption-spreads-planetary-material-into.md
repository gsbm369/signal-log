Asymptotic Giant Branch (AGB) stars spread heavy elements into space with their stellar winds. This material eventually forms more stars, and even rocky planets like ours. For a long time, astronomers thought this important mass-loss was driven by stellar winds. But new research shows how magnetic fields play an important role. A vast flare and magnetic loop of material from the AGB star Mira provides the evidence.
