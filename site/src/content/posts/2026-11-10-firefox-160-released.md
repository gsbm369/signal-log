Version 160 of the Firefox web browser has been released. Notable features in this release include the ability to block sites from asking to handle " mailto: " links, ability to play Matroska files with FLAC, MP3, or PCM audio, playback speed controls in the picture-in-picture video player, and a new Mini Window feature that is in early development.
