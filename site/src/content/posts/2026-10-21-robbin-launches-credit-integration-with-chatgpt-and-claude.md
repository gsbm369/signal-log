Robbin is launching Latin America's first credit MCP, a technology that lets finance teams work with the company's credit intelligence directly inside the AI assistants they already use. Integrated with Claude and ChatGPT, it turns credit analysis into a chat: limit recommendations for a given customer, risk assessments across the buyer base, and flags on limits that need review.
