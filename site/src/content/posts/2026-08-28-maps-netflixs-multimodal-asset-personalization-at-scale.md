Every visual cue, from the artwork on a title to the video previews that autoplay while you browse, is there to connect you with a story you will love. We call these visual cues assets , and choosing the right one for each member is a personalization problem of its own. But which image or video preview of Squid Game should we show you? And what do we do right after a title launches, when there’s far too little interaction data to know which asset we should recommend to each member? For years, our models answered the first question well and the second poorly.
