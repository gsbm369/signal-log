The FCC has authorized SpaceX to deploy up to 1 million Starmind satellites to process AI data as orbital data centers. SpaceX is targeting its first launches in 2027, and the full constellation is set to be operating by 2040. Google and Blue Origin are also working on AI infrastructure in space, but Boston Consulting Group estimates costs will stay well above ground-based data centers for now.
