A comment on a lobste.rs post about the tech culture stagnating made me think. 20-15 or even 10 years ago, tech was really exciting. Sure, monopolies were bad, I was fighting them, but tech by itself was very interesting. New versions of software were expected, we were skimming through changelogs to find novelties, installing beta or alpha versions of totally unknown projects. And I had lived that excitement since the 90’s. When you think about it, today feels really bleak in comparison: Changelogs of commercial projects now all have that pseudo-humorous tone to tell you that "they improved performance and fixed bugs" The best smartphone I ever had is now 10 years old (and was not an expensive one, see https://ploum.net/2026-09-02-i_dont_have_a_smartphone.html ) Around me, at least 3 totally unrelated people left a decade-long career in tech to do woodworking. And they are way happier. M
