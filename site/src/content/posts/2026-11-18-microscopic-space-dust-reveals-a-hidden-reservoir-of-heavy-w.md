Astronomers can find valuable information in even the tiniest objects. That was certainly the case recently when twelve dust grains collected by a high-altitude aircraft helped solve a mystery about the birth of our Solar System. Or at least that is the argument in a new paper published in Science Advances by researchers at NASA’s Johnson Space Center and UCLA. According to the paper, the region beyond Neptune once had a massive isolated reservoir of “heavy water” that never mixed in with the inner planets.
