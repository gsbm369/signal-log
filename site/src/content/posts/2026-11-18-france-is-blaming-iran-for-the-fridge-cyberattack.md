Interesting : U.S. intelligence agencies have recently reached a low confidence assessment that Iran was responsible for the attacks in France, according to three U.S. officials who spoke on the condition of anonymity to discuss sensitive intelligence. A low confidence assessment generally means that there is some intelligence that points in a certain direction but is fragmented, and that the assessment could change as more information is collected. The agencies judgment on the French incidents linked those attacks to Iran generally and not to a specific group such as the Revolutionary Guards, two of the officials said. They added that Iran was also seen as probably responsible for other similar attacks around Europe, but that those investigations were in their early stages...
