The potentially historic vote at GTA 6 creators Rockstar Games on whether to seek formal union recognition has failed, the International Workers of Great Britain have announced. Around 1600 studio members cast ballots across 12th to 26th November , even as the company's open world Bonnie & Clyde 'em up released onto PlayStation and Xbox storefronts. In the end, approximately 25% of the participants voted in favour. According to the IWGB themselves, "the result does not prevent Rockstar workers from organising with the IWGB or exercising their trade union rights, but means management will not be formally committed to regular meetings with union representatives."
