Introduction Artificial intelligence (AI) coding assistants have changed how fast we can ship. They have not changed how mobile users experience a crash. That gap is the pressure every mobile stability program now operates under. The Grab superapp, which millions of users across Southeast Asia use to book rides, order food, and more, operates at a large scale. On Android alone, it spans millions of lines of code , with many engineers shipping code into every release on a weekly release cycle . Maintaining app stability is not just a technical goal; it is a product commitment to those users. With the rise of AI coding assistance, the volume of change in every release has grown, and so has the number of ways a single change can break something in an already large codebase.
