The Rust All Hands 2027 will again take place in Utrecht, the Netherlands as part of RustWeek 2027 , hosted by RustNL . The RustWeek conference takes place on Tuesday (25 May) and Wednesday (26 May) followed by the three-day Rust All Hands on Thursday (27 May), Friday (28 May) and Saturday (29 May) ! The second conference day (Wednesday 26 May) will again include a "Rust Project Track", where Rust team members can give talks to other team members and current and future contributors. The Rust All Hands is a private event for people affiliated with the Rust project. This could be Rust team membership, participation in a working group, owning a project goal, or similar involvement in the future of the Rust project.
