As Earth's surface area grew, weathering fed more nutrients into Earth's ancient oceans. This eventually resulted in more oxygen, making Earth more habitable. New research identifies two ancient mountain growth spurts on Earth that directly preceded two of Earth's oxygenation events. The researchers say that taller mountains encouraged habitability and led to complex life.
