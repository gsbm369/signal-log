Skipper lets you express multi-step business processes as Java or Kotlin classes, persist their progress, and recover from crashes and transient failures. It runs inside your service and reuses its existing dependencies. Skipper is available under the Apache 2.0 license. The code is on GitHub , alongside documentation and runnable examples.
