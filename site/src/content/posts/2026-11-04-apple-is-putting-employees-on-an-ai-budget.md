Apple is curtailing employee AI use to cut costs, reports Bloomberg . In October, Apple limited some engineers to 200 per day for AI spending, down from 300. More cuts could be coming, with daily allowances for some engineers dropping to 100. Many groups outside engineering already have a 100 daily limit, but some teams could have their spending capped at 25. Apple is asking some teams to use less expensive models or switch to on-device AI. Employees who need a bigger budget will be able to ask executives for an increase. Engineers at Apple have the largest budgets because using AI for coding can consume a large number of tokens. Apple lets employees use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other models.
