LLMs as Parts of Systems Towers of Hanoi is a boring game, anyway. Over on the Kiro blog, I wrote a post about Kiro and the future of AI spec-driven software development , looking at where I think the space of AI-agent-powered development tools is going. In that post, I made a bit of cheeky oblique reference to a topic I think is super important. I asked Kiro to build a Towers of Hanoi game. It’s an oblique reference to Apple’s The Illusion of Thinking paper, and the discourse that followed it. The question of whether LLMs can scalably play Towers of Hanoi is an interesting theoretically and scientifically, but not the most important question. The more important one is can systems built with LLMs play these games? . By picking me Towers of Hanoi in that other post, I was pointing out that the answer is clearly yes .
