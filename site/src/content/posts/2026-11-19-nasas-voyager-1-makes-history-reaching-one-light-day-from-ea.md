NASA’s Voyager 1 probe, humanity’s farthest-flying spacecraft, has reached one light-day from Earth, after more than 49 years of constant travel. A light-day is the distance light travels in 24 hours, equaling about 16.1 billion miles (25.9 billion kilometers). The spacecraft achieved the milestone at 2:16 a.m. PST (5:16 a.m. EST) Nov. 18.
