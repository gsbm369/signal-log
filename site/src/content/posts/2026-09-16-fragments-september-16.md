Reports of agentic hacking continue, in this case it happened back in May and it seems OpenAI did not disclose that they were responsible. Simon Willison sees two options: After the Hugging Face and Wiki attacks OpenAI were still unable to review their previous logs and determine that they had previously attacked RubyGems. They knew about the attack on RubyGems and made the decision not to reach out to the RubyGems team about it. Both of these are bad! Given this incident, the Hugging Face situation, and the Wiki attack, the obvious question right now is how many more incidents like this are out there waiting to be discovered? ❄ ❄ ❄ ❄ ❄ Dave Farley: Stop asking the sci-fi question: ‘Is it conscious?’ Start asking the engineering question: ‘Is this a powerful, unpredictable component being put somewhere consequential, and where’s the feedback that tells us that it’s safe? ❄ ❄ ❄ ❄ ❄ Nate S
