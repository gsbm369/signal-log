Extremely good King's Field-style RPG Queen's Domain or is it a Metroid Prime homage? Or a stealth Morrowind-up? We hacks simply cannot decide has received its first proper patch, adding a hitherto "missing" throwing weapon, the Grave Knife, alongside a bunch of quality-of-life fixes. They've stirred in the ability to disable screenshake, alongside some new icons to make status effects more obvious. They've also introduced "options to make some late game puzzles less frustrating", though they don't specify which. How frustrating!
