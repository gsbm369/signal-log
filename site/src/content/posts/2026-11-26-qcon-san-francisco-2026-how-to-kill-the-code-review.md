Ankit Jain of Aviator told QCon San Francisco 2026 that line-by-line code review is already fading, since most review comments concern knowledge sharing rather than defects and over 75% of PRs merge without human review. He proposed six layers: argue, capture, codify, prove, debate, own. His team merges four times more PRs, with about 40% fewer reverts. By Daniel Curtis
