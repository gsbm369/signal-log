Astronomers have found a Long Period Radio Transient about 5,000 light years away that's different from other LPTs. Other LPTs have a white dwarf and a red dwarf, but this one has a white dwarf and a main sequence star. The exact cause of the binary pair's radio pulses isn't certain, but this work narrows it down. It also shows that these binaries could be common progenitors for Type Ia supernova explosions.
