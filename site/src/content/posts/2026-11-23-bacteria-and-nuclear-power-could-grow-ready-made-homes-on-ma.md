As we gear up to eventually land a human on Mars, more and more ideas are coming out about how to prepare for that. A recent one from Pioneer Labs, a research institute founded by bioengineer Erika DeBenedictis to develop biological technologies for space exploration, is now available on arXiv, without any plans to release it in a paid journal as part of the “Open Science” policy of The Astera Institute, who funded the research. It details “Mars Seed” - a new architecture that is self-contained and can produce a full 870 sq meters of pressurized habitat using nothing but Martian inputs within roughly every four Earth years.
