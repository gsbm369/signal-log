At Payments Unleashed in London, Tom Warsop, CEO, ACI Worldwide joined FinextraTV to share his insights into where payments are heading and what the future will look like. Warsop describes the benefits of a forum to focus on how to progress payments towards a new future. He explained how the industry has a tendency to focus on the 'shiny new toy' and how payments are not forgiving, so execution has to be watertight. He describes the unfortunate reality of how conceptualisation is a lot easier than reality, and payments often runs into the struggles of this realisation - the hard work begins when the conceptual part is down and it's turned into a reality.
