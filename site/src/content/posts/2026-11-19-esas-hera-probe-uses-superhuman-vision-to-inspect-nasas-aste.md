Around four years ago, humanity intentionally hit an asteroid. The Double Asteroid Redirection Test (DART) was by pretty much all measures a resounding success when it struck the asteroid moonlet Dimorphos going more than 22,000 kilometers per hour. However, we still don’t know just how much of a success it actually was - DART was a one-way mission with no real way to track the results of the impact close up. Enter Hera, the European Space Agency (ESA) mission that recently arrived in the binary asteroid system. Now, in a press release, ESA has provided some of the best pictures of the binary system since that fateful day four years ago.
