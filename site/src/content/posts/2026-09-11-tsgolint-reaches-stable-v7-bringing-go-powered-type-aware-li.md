tsgolint has released a stable v7, enhancing TypeScript linting with native Go speed. It offers type-aware linting, leveraging TypeScript's semantic analysis through the typescript-go compiler. Oxlint manages configurations and file discovery. The release, compatible with TypeScript 7.0.2, handles 59 of 61 type-aware rules and shows significant performance improvements over ESLint.
