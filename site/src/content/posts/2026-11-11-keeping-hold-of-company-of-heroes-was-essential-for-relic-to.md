In the nearly 30 years since they opened their doors, Relic have been an enormously influential studio. Responsible for lauded RTS series Homeworld, Dawn of War, and Company of Heroes , they also dabbled in other genres with third-person shooter The Outfit and the original Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine. Yet, despite this history, in 2024, when Relic were negotiating their sale from Sega to UK investment firm Emona Capita , CEO Justin Dowdeswell knew the rights to Company of Heroes had to be part of the deal. "Without it we would have been a dead studio," he tells me. Without naming names Dowdeswell points out that there are "numerous examples" of studios that weren't able to survive on their name alone.
