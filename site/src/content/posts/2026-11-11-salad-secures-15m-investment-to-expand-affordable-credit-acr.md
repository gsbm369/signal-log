Salad, the UK’s largest consumer lending Community Development Finance Institution (CDFI), has secured a £1.5 million social impact investment from City Bridge Foundation, enabled by a £1.2 million funding guarantee from Fair4All Finance. The partnership will unlock up to £20 million in affordable and responsible lending capacity, helping more Londoners access fair credit when they are unable to do so through mainstream providers.
