New research shows that star formation is occurring in the Milky Way's central bar. This goes against previous research, and against observations of other barred spiral galaxies. The discovery by a PhD student shows a vast rotating structure of stars near the centre of our Galaxy is driving enormous shock fronts through clouds of gas, compressing them and creating conditions for new stars to form.
