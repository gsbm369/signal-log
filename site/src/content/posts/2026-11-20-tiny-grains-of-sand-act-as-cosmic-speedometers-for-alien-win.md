We’ve managed to take high-resolution orbital images of many worlds across our solar system, and many of them with an atmosphere have one feature in common - sand dunes. Scientists have been using the size and orientation of these dunes for decades to act like a weather vane, figuring out which way the wind blows on these other worlds. Unfortunately, weather vanes aren’t good at telling the full windy story - they can’t tell you how fast the wind is going. However, according to a new paper published in the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences by researchers at Stanford, sand dunes can.
