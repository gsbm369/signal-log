Apple is releasing five new products this Friday, November 6, following pre-orders that opened last week , including its first touchscreen MacBook Pro models. The following new products will be available from Friday: iPad mini with OLED display and A20 Pro chip iMac with M6 chip 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 chip 14-inch MacBook Pro with OLED touchscreen 16-inch MacBook Pro with OLED touchscreen The iPad mini starts at 699, the iMac starts at 1,499, and the M6 MacBook Pro at 1,999. Pricing for the MacBook Pro with OLED touchscreen starts at 2,899 for the 14-inch model and 3,399 for the 16-inch model. All five remain available to pre-order, with shipping dates generally looking good for launch-day delivery.
