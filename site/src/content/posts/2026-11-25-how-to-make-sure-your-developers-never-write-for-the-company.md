Bring up writing blogs just once a year at your offsite. Make it clear you don't want writing now but after the next big release is out. Make sure writing blogs is seen as the function of the marketing team. When you get a draft, mark it all up with Claude comments. Rewrite it with Claude generally. Make it well understood there is a long and drawn out process to getting published. Make frequent changes to your website without looking at how code or technical articles look on the site, or if they even render at all. Have external consultants rewrite sections of published articles for SEO. Make sure not to ask the author for approval or let them know it happened. Do not reward publishing. VPs and directors, let alone founders, should not proudly share these posts on social media.
