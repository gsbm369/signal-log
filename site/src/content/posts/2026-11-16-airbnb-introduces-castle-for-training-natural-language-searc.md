Airbnb researchers have introduced CASTLE, a framework designed to address the cold-start problem in natural language search. The approach uses de-identified booking sessions and large language models (LLMs) to jointly generate synthetic search queries and relevance labels, enabling model training before sufficient real search traffic is available.
