Kristof Beyls has published a summary to the LLVM discussion forum to announce that the project is declaring success and bringing its relicensing effort to a close after eight years. We've been asking past contributors to relicense their work since 2018. New contributions have come in under Apache-2.0 WITH LLVM-exception since January 19, 2019. Code contributed before that date was under LLVM's earlier licenses (UIUC/NCSA, plus MIT for some runtime libraries), so we needed permission from its authors to move it over. [...] Today, more than 99.5% of all lines of code ever contributed to LLVM are either covered by the new license, too small for copyright to apply, or no longer in the repository.
