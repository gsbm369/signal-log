As you probably know, I am obsessed with examples. Really , really , really obsessed with examples. The best examples should at least: Be simple enough to understand Clearly showcase the topic under discussion Actually correspond to how the topic manifests in the real world Give some rough idea on how to find other examples in the same vein Basically, the example needs to both help the student understand the topic and also why the topic matters. If not to them, at least to someone they know. A good example of a good example is demonstrating type safety with option types, which ensure that you always handle nulls in your program.
