It's their first model to use a looped transformer architecture, which they describe like this: In a standard Transformer, each layer runs once per forward pass. In a Loop Transformer, a group of weight-shared layers runs for multiple iterations, refining the hidden states from the previous pass. This gives us a deeper network without increasing the parameter count.
