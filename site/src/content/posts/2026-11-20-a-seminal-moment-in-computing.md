MG, founder and CEO of WorkOS, is back to talk about leading a company through what DHH called a seminal moment in computing. He explains Atlas, the AI coworker WorkOS built for itself and then launched, why agents will soon choose and sign up for your tech stack, and why this moment feels closer to fire than to the iPhone. Then he gets practical: starting a company is easier than ever, but product-market fit is harder, so be a heat-seeking missile for pain, pick customers you like, and stop calling yourself non-technical.
