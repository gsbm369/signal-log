SBI Holdings, Inc. (Head Office: Minato-ku, Tokyo; Representative Director, Chairman, President & CEO: Yoshitaka Kitao; hereinafter “SBI Holdings”) hereby announces that it has entered into a Memorandum of Understanding with CRB Group, Inc. (Head Office: New Jersey, U.S.; Chairman & CEO: Gilles Gade; hereinafter “CRB Group”), the parent company of Cross River Bank (hereinafter “Cross River”), a leading technology infrastructure platform powering embedded financial solutions serving as the trust anchor for global banking and money movement, regarding a strategic business alliance and the proposed establishment of a joint venture in Japan.
