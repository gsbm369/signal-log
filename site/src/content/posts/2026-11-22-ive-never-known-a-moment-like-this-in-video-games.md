It was hard not to spend the first hour or so of Grand Theft Auto 6 's launch day in a kind of stun-lock. As far as video games editorial goes - which is not that far, in fairness; prepare for an over-flattering analogy - the effect was roughly the equivalent of Tom Hanks' experience, as his John H. Miller first set foot on Omaha beach. Tweets and reels whistled overhead, news leads stung the ears, exploding search terms blew apart some poor guides team ally on one side, while new discoveries in the early game - made by the millions and millions of launch-day players all picking it apart at once - appeared by surprise like some flanking ambush on the other.
