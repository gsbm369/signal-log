Marcus Borg recounts an experiment to investigate if healthy code was cheaper for agents to review . Between a typical unhealthy pull request (CodeHealth 2.8) and a typical healthy one (9.7), that adds up to 22 to 35%. The measures of health come from CodeScene. The experiment looked at three LLMs, 465 pull requests on 246 open source repositories. Each pull request was put through a review and fix pipeline involving five agents. They also looked at which code smells led to increased costs: they highlighted Complex Method and Complex Conditional from the smells identified by CodeScene.
