Token fraud is here and it’s BAD (YouTube) Claire Vo runs ChatPRD , an AI platform that helps product managers "turn an idea into a spec". The product has been live since the GPT-4 era, and Claire has been building it in public for much of that time. In this YouTube video (see also the accompanying Twitter post ) Claire describes the flood of token fraud she has seen from groups abusing the product's free trial, sometimes just to get free AI usage but often to resell tokens or run other commercial enterprises on top of the ChatPRD agent.
