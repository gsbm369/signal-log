Euclid was launched to build a 3D map of the sky to probe cosmic structure and the Universe's expansion. Part of its job is to hunt for strong gravitational lenses, important probes of dark matter. So far it's off to a great start. It found 15,000 strong gravitational lens candidates in its first data relase.
