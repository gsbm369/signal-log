Kerem Söke has announced the 1.0 release of Polo , a Wayland compositor for the MATE Desktop Environment : The idea is to keep MATE behaving as closely as possible to how it does on X11, rather than changing the desktop to fit a different compositor. [...] Window management, metacity, workspaces, tiling, XWayland, clipboard and clipman support, multiple monitors, display scaling, hotplugging, screenshots, screen sharing, and session management are all working. I've also added desktop effects: fade, zoom, zap, spin, squeemize, magic lamp, desktop cube, expo, and wobbly windows. Söke is looking for people to test Polo, particularly on multi-monitor setups, and report back with problems.
