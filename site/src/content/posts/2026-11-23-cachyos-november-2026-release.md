CachyOS , a performance-focused distribution based on Arch Linux , has announced its November 2026 release. Notable changes in this release include a new recovery environment ("cachyos-recovery") for rolling back broken systems, Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC) v2 support for games that require EAC to run, version 2.0.0 of the CachyOS Package Installer, and more.
