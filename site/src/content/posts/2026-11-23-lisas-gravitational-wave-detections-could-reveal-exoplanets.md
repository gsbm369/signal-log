LISA, the ESA's Laser Interferometry Space Antenna, is being built to detect gravitational waves emitted when massive objects spiral together and merge. New research shows that exoplanets can exert a small but perceptible tug on those objects, leaving a signature on the gravitational waves. In some cases, LISA should be able to detect their signatures.
