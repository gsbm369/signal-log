Late last week, reports emerged of some folks having Nvidia's latest GeForce driver, 621.24, needlessly write vast amounts of data to their PC's SSD, a bug that could affect the component's lifespan at a time when replacing SSDs certainly isn't cheap . The immediate short-term solution for affected users was to roll back to a previous driver, 621.01. Sadly it appears that might still be the best way to go despite Nvidia having released a hotfix.
