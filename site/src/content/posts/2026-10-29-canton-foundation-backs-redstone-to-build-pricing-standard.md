RedStone, the data layer for institutional DeFi, today announced that the Canton Foundation has unanimously approved funding through the Canton Development Fund to develop the Canton Access and Privacy Standard (CAPS), an open-source framework for institutional market-data infrastructure on Canton. CAPS is designed to let institutions price transactions on Canton without revealing their underlying positions or agreed settlement values to data providers.
