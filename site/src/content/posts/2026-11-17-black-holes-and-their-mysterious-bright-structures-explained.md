Black hole jets from the SMBH in Centaurus A, and from some other SMBH, are brighter on their edges than in their centers. It's called limb brightening, and it's unexpected, and astrophysicists want to know what causes it. They found their answer in detailed plasma physics simulations, and the results could also help efforts to develop fusion power.
