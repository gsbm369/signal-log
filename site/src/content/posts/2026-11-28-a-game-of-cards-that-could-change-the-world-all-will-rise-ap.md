I'm a big fan of the moment when, years into a gaming subgenre's existence, people start rethinking what those games are capable of. All the expected ideas, they've been done, so the latecomers are by necessity inventors if they want to stand out. All Will Rise is one of them. In essence, it's a deck-building card game that was volcanoed into existence by the popularity of Slay the Spire several years ago, or perhaps Magic: The Gathering before, or perhaps Pok mon.
